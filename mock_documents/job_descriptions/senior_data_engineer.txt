# Senior Data Engineer - Data Platform Team
- Position: Data Engineer
- Level: Senior
- Team: Data Platform Team
- Location: Remote (US/Canada) or Hybrid (San Francisco, CA)
- Employment Type: Full-time

## About the Role:
Join our Data Platform Team as a Senior Data Engineer and help build and maintain our data infrastructure. You'll be responsible for designing and implementing scalable data pipelines, ensuring data quality, and enabling data-driven decision making across the organization.

## Requirements:
- 5+ years of experience in data engineering or related field
- Expert-level proficiency in Python and SQL
- Deep knowledge of data warehousing concepts and technologies
- Experience with big data technologies (Spark, Hadoop, etc.)
- Strong understanding of ETL/ELT processes and data modeling
- Experience with cloud data platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Knowledge of data quality and testing frameworks
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
- Excellent problem-solving and communication skills

## Responsibilities:
- Design and implement scalable data pipelines and ETL processes
- Architect and maintain data warehouse solutions
- Develop and optimize data models for analytics
- Implement data quality checks and monitoring
- Collaborate with data scientists and analysts
- Mentor junior data engineers
- Drive technical decisions and best practices
- Participate in on-call rotation and incident response
- Contribute to technical documentation

## Nice to Have:
- Experience with real-time data processing
- Knowledge of machine learning pipelines
- Familiarity with data governance and security
- Experience with containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)
- Understanding of data visualization tools
- Contributions to open-source data projects
- Experience with streaming data technologies (Kafka, etc.)
