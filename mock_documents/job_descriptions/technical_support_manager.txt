# Technical Support Manager - Customer Success Team
- Position: Technical Support Manager
- Level: Manager
- Team: Customer Success Team
- Location: Remote (US/Canada) or Hybrid (San Francisco, CA)
- Employment Type: Full-time

## About the Role:
Join our Customer Success Team as a Technical Support Manager and lead our technical support operations. You'll be responsible for managing a team of support engineers, ensuring high-quality customer service, and driving continuous improvement in our support processes.

## Requirements:
- 5+ years of experience in technical support or customer service
- 2+ years of experience managing technical support teams
- Strong technical background with ability to understand complex systems
- Experience with support ticketing systems (Zendesk, Jira, etc.)
- Knowledge of customer service best practices and metrics
- Excellent leadership and team management skills
- Strong problem-solving and decision-making abilities
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
- Outstanding communication and interpersonal skills

## Responsibilities:
- Lead and mentor a team of technical support engineers
- Develop and implement support processes and best practices
- Monitor and improve support metrics and KPIs
- Handle escalations and complex customer issues
- Collaborate with product and engineering teams
- Create and maintain support documentation
- Conduct regular team meetings and performance reviews
- Drive customer satisfaction and retention
- Participate in on-call rotation for critical issues

## Nice to Have:
- Experience with SaaS products and services
- Knowledge of ITIL or similar service management frameworks
- Familiarity with customer success platforms
- Understanding of data analytics and reporting
- Experience with remote team management
- Knowledge of support automation tools
- Background in software development or system administration
